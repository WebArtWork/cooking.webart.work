# World Classic Recipes 601–800 — English / Ukrainian

200 dishes across 46 African country/territory groups. `world-0601` to `world-0800`.

## File structure

- `en/601.json` through `en/800.json`: one English `RecipeDraft` per file
- `ua/601.json` through `ua/800.json`: matching Ukrainian `RecipeDraft` records
- `recipes.en.json` and `recipes.ua.json`: all per-language records in arrays
- `manifest.json`: counts, codes, provisional regions, substitutions and QA limitations

Every recipe follows the provided interfaces exactly. Ingredient amounts appear **only** on the step using them; `techniqueIds` and `ingredientId` values resolve against the supplied catalogs; `unit` resolves against `units.json`.

## Editorial notes

The region IDs are provisional slugs, not proven app REGIONS IDs. Recipes are home-scale, research-informed editorial drafts, not kitchen-tested. **Do not publish as verified traditional recipes without regional culinary and food-safety review**. Especially compare the adaptation map for 620 (brik pastry), 640 (kisra fermentation), 668 (banku), 672 (kenkey), 673 (attiéké), 688 (cassava leaves absent), 705 (kilishi), 725 (odika), 740 (cooked kitfo), and 797 (boerewors without casings).

**Safety**: Dried kidney beans require sufficient boiling prior to simmering; raw cassava leaves require specialized processing and are not represented here. Do not treat cured or dried meat as shelf-stable without validated food safety controls. Separate clean ingredients from raw-meat marinade; cook chicken and minced meats fully and check with appropriate food thermometers.
