# Classic world recipes 201–400 — EN / UA

200 distinct dishes and 400 localized RecipeDraft JSON records. Stable IDs `world-0201`…`world-0400`.

- `en/201.json`…`en/400.json`, `ua/201.json`…`ua/400.json`: individual localized recipes.
- `recipes.en.json`, `recipes.ua.json`: equivalent bulk arrays.
- `manifest.json`: counts, coverage, validation and limitations.
- Ingredient references, technique references and units all use catalog IDs.
- Recipe-level ingredient lists are intentionally absent; every ingredient belongs to a step.
- `regionId` provisional `southern-europe` / `western-and-northern-europe`; replace from app REGIONS if needed.
- Kosovo is encoded as `XK` (widely used, but **not** an officially assigned ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code); adapt to the application's country registry before import.

## Editorial notes
Recipes are researched home-kitchen drafts rather than kitchen-tested authoritative formulas. Exact regional products missing from the ingredient catalog have documented adaptation notes in descriptions; examples include squid ink for Dalmatian black risotto, whole fermented cabbage leaves for sarma, traditional caul fat for sheftalia, and fermented trahana. For genuine canonical recipes, extend the ingredient catalog and revise the affected records.

For fresh foods, food safety depends on temperature, times, handling, and individual product conditions; validate these before publication. Salt-cod desalting requires refrigeration and multiple water changes; dried red kidney beans need an appropriate rapid boil before simmering, and mussels must be cooked until open and closed shells discarded.
