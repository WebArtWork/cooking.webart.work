Baking Institute

About the school

The institute runs short, practical courses in bread, pastry and desserts. Classes are small, and every student bakes at their own station.

Programme

  1. Bread

    Yeast, sourdough and shaping.

  2. Pastry

    Shortcrust, laminated dough and choux.

  3. Desserts

    Creams, custards and plating.

Admission

Duration
1 to 6 weeks, evenings and weekends
City
Lviv

No experience needed for the beginner courses.
